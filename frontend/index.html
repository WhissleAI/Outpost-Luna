Outpost Luna

A voice game for kids 7 to 12. No crew has landed at the Moon's south pole yet. Train at Flight School, then run Outpost Luna, a base there.

How you play

Ten days on the Moon, one shift at a time

  • Pick a site. Each one trades sunlight, ice and how often you can see Earth.
  • Balance three things. Power, life support and radiation dose.
  • Talk to CAPCOM. "Shields to 40." "Send the rover for ice." "End shift." (The shields are pretend tech.)
  • Call Houston. Radio takes about 1.3 seconds each way, so you wait for the answer.
  • Debrief. See your timeline and what worked. Then try another plan.
  • Library. Explore the Moon, the hardware left there, and the Artemis II crew's own words.
Flight School

Train first, then command

  • Short lessons with CAPCOM. Moon phases and eclipses, gravity and dust, the south pole, radio talk, solar power, radiation, air and ice, and lessons from Apollo.
  • Answer your way. Voice, typing or tapping. Hints come when you're stuck, and sooner after a long pause. Facts show their sources.
  • Check ride. Two days on the simulator, with a storm based on the real May 2024 storm that gave Earth huge auroras. Pass it to earn the Mission Commander patch.
  • Saved here. Patches and your call sign stay on this device.

The story is inspired by NASA's Artemis program. Artemis I flew uncrewed in 2022. Artemis II flew its crew around the Moon in April 2026. Artemis III, planned for 2027, is a crewed test flight in Earth orbit. The first crew landing is planned for Artemis IV in 2028, and NASA hasn't picked the site yet.

Built on NASA open data, plus a few science papers. Some outpost tech is pretend, and we label it. CAPCOM and Houston are played by the game.

Privacy

Stays on this computer

Your settings stay on this device too. Speech is understood by Whissle's Arc models running locally, not in the cloud.

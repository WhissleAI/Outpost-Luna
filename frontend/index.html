About

Ask the Moon

A voice Moon explorer for kids. Ask out loud, and the Moon turns to show you the places, and the machines people left there.

The challenge

Abandoned but not forgotten

NASA Space Apps 2026 asks for stories about the equipment left behind on the Moon and Mars. Landers, rovers, impact sites and instruments are still out there, and each one has a story.

What it does

Ghost hardware you can talk to

  • Find it. Glowing diamonds on the 3D Moon mark the hardware, coloured by kind: lander, rover, impact, instrument, memorial.
  • Hear it. Tap one and it tells its own story, in its own voice. More facts, the crew and the sources are one tap away.
  • Ask it. "Who drove the Apollo 16 rover?" The guide answers, flies there, and can take you on a tour.
  • Listen to the crew. Real Artemis II recordings play while the globe follows the places the astronauts name.
  • Missions. Find-it clues; tap the Moon to answer.
How it works

Whissle's Arc models, running locally

Arc Tagger reads every question for places, numbers and private details; names and addresses are caught and never looked up. Arc audio metadata listens for when a kid starts and stops talking. Both run on a local model server, not in the cloud. Every fact the guide says is checked against a fact sheet built from the sources below.

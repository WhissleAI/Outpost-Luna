About

Outpost Luna

A voice game for kids 7 to 12. Train at Flight School, then run Outpost Luna, a base at the Moon's south pole.

The story

Train for the first south-pole landing

Artemis I flew uncrewed in 2022. Artemis II flew its crew around the Moon in April 2026. Artemis III is a 2027 crewed test flight in Earth orbit, and the first landing near the south pole is planned for Artemis IV. You train to help run Outpost Luna for the crews who come after.

How you play

Ten days on the Moon, one shift at a time

  • Flight School. Short lessons on the Moon map. Say, type or tap your answers. Earn a patch for each one, then fly a check ride.
  • Pick a site. Each one trades sunlight, ice and how often you can see Earth.
  • Balance three things. Power, life support and radiation dose.
  • Talk to CAPCOM. "Shields to 40." "Send the rover for ice." "End shift."
  • Call Houston. Radio takes about 1.3 seconds each way, so you wait for the answer.
  • Debrief. See your timeline and what worked. Then try another plan.
  • Library. Explore the Moon, the hardware left there, and the Artemis II crew's own words.
What's real

Real data, some game rules

The Moon map, the sites, the Sun and Earth in the sky and the solar storms come from NASA open data and published space science. Some rules are made up so the game works, like how much power shields use. The Debrief shows which. CAPCOM and Houston are played by the game.

Privacy

Stays on this computer

Your call sign, patches and settings are saved only on this device. Speech is understood by Whissle's Arc models running locally, not in the cloud.

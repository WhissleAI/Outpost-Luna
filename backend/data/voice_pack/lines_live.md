# Voice pack: what stays live

Generated by `make voice-pack-lines` (backend/tools/voice_pack_lines.py). The pack holds 1145 lines (1117 capcom, 28 guide); everything below is spoken live by the OS voice engine.

Always live, by design:

- **Houston** and the **hardware's story voice**: they keep their own role voices.
- **Vocabulary lines** (Space words, a glossary word's "hear it"): slowed and stressed on purpose.
- **Answers**: the Director's replies, place facts, tours, crew-clip intros.
- Any line at a pace other than 1x, or with the recorded guide voice turned off in Settings, or with a chosen voice other than the default guide voice (docs/VOICE_PACK.md).

# Teacher demo: 15 minutes (Colorado high-school archaeology teacher)

**Goal:** show how a lesson plays, what she would see as the teacher, and that nothing leaves the computer. Then listen.

**What must be built first:** the demo cut in `docs/COURSES.md` §5:
- P1 and P2;
- lessons `what_is`, `layers`, `dating`, and the Chimney Rock bridge;
- Survey season chapters 1–2, labelled "Preview".

**Setup:**
- One laptop with the installed app.
- The Archaeology course pack already downloaded.
- A cadet profile named "Demo".
- School network mode **on**, to show it.
- Sound on for her (this is the room demo, not a test run).

| Time | What you do | What she sees and hears | The point |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open the app on the Moon map. Say "switch course". Pick Archaeology. | The Moon turns into Earth and flies to south-west Colorado: the ten lesson pins fan out round Mesa Verde (zoom out and they fold into one "10" pin). | One app, many courses. Each course downloads once and works offline. |
| 1:00 | Open the explorer lesson *Moonrise at Chimney Rock*. | The Moon, then Earth, then Chimney Rock. "Every 18.6 years…" | The Moon course and the Archaeology course meet at a real Colorado site. |
| 2:00 | Lesson 4 *Dating the past*, by voice only. | Predict, then the tree-ring match (`rings`), then "1275", then the Long House beam fact with its NPS source. | Real data (the ITRDB Mesa Verde series), real dates, a source on every fact. |
| 5:00 | Lesson 3 *Layers tell time*: the dig. | Order the layers; the intrusive-pit question. A wrong answer gets a hint, not a buzzer. | Inquiry: evidence, then claim. Hints after two misses. |
| 8:00 | Survey season, chapters 1–2 (Preview). | The Now card says "Write to the tribes' cultural offices" before any digging. Then lidar at low sun. | Ethics and consultation are part of the method, not a side lesson. |
| 11:00 | Settings → For grown-ups (hold 3 s) → Progress. | The Demo cadet's Archaeology section: lessons, patches, first-try counts, the Colorado standards list. Press Print. | What a teacher files. No accounts. |
| 12:00 | For grown-ups → Class lessons → New lesson, on Archaeology. | She writes a 3-step lesson about her own field trip, previews it, and saves it. | Her course, her lessons; class packs attach to a course. |
| 13:00 | For grown-ups → School network. | "On: nothing is contacted." | Privacy: on the device; nothing is sent; Reset erases it. |
| 13:30 | Questions. | — | — |

**Ask her** (write the answers into `docs/COURSES.md` §7):
1. Which units does your course have, and in what order?
2. Which Colorado standard codes does your course plan cite?
3. What do students struggle with most: dating, stratigraphy, or the law?
4. Would you introduce us to Crow Canyon's education team, or a tribal cultural office, to review Units 6–8?
5. Would one class try it for two weeks? On which computers, and with what network rules?

**Do not:**
- show coordinates for any site below park level;
- show or describe human remains;
- say "Anasazi" or "vanished";
- claim a tribe's endorsement we do not have.
